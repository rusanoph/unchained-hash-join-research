# Workload recovery

The frozen manifest comes from the original executed plans and records their SHA-256 alongside the historical observation hashes. The 35 original SNAP cases and 18 holdout instances have disjoint graph datasets. Within each graph suite, delivered and permuted forms remain identifiable variants of the same graph and query pattern. The original includes two-hop, filtered two-hop, three-hop, stars and triangles. The holdout includes three two-input patterns on Enron, HepPh and Amazon0302 under two orderings. Its 12 Enron/HepPh cases and six Amazon03 controls remain separately identifiable.

The independent SNAP checks use the existing DuckDB CLI on the original Parquet. The [oracle](../workloads/graph-oracle.json) covers all 53 instances. It reproduces the 18 archived holdout results. It also records logical-left and logical-right frequencies for 49 binary joins and tests the identity `M = sum(f_left(k) * f_right(k))` against actual query output counts. It keeps multi-join output counts and checksums for the other four cases. The frequency overlap coefficient is `sum(min(f_left(k)/L, f_right(k)/R))`. A physical-plan build/probe swap changes the denominator of fanout and the interpretation of build multiplicity. Check the physical aliases when using these statistics.

The JOB archive has 100 published cases and 13 separately registered exclusions. The tested statements match the archived numbered SQL files. All 113 map by query ID and SQL tokens to the pinned upstream JOB suite after ignoring whitespace and casing outside quotes. The validation uses the original statements and archived Parquet bytes. The [input receipt](../workloads/input-receipt.json) has the full query mapping. The 13 exclusions are the queries outside the published timing cohort, including `22d`. Their earlier historical coverage and failure details can be recomputed from the recorded benchmark archives.

## Historical loss cases

Use the [historical medians](../workloads/historical.json) for cohort selection. The strongest v10b JOB declines by point estimate were 7a and 4b. For 7a, the original light-run native and auto medians were approximately 295 and 317 ms. Its native A/A reference was about 293 ms. At least one approximately 348K-row build selected HashCSR with an estimated 833K-row probe. The shorter 4b case took approximately 25.9 and 27.4 ms across the same arms and showed no selected contiguous build in the sampled historical observation. Treat gate/phase costs and A/A noise as separate explanations until the current metrics discriminate them. Earlier v6c one-observation JOB results included materially negative point estimates for 2d and 29a. They are diagnostic cases under a different adapter and measurement protocol. The current A/B evidence must establish each shape and effect afresh.
